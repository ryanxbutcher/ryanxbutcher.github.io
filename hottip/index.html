HOT TIP

Open late · iPhone · Free

A tip calculator that happens to have a cast.

Type the bill. Read the answer. That’s the whole app — unless you look up, in which case somebody is working in there and has opinions about your 5%.

Tip Pete 25% and he’s suspicious. Tip him 2% and he’s delighted.

Six rooms. Six people with views.

Pick one and somebody is working in it. Tip well and they say so. Tip badly and they say that too. Nobody is ever mean to you about money you don’t have.

  • The LoungeMandy meets everyone at the door. She has heard it.
  • The DinerMustang Sally writes everything down. Everything.
  • The Blue NoteTia keeps the cleanest books in the house.
  • The AbductionHanahhh came for a specimen and stayed for the drinks.
  • The Traveling MujerElizabeth counts your change back to you.
  • The Open RoadPete’s scale runs backwards. He’s proud of a small tip.

Nobody is going to explain any of this.

The six of them know each other. They will not tell you how, and they do not agree about it. Stay a while and you will start picking it up sideways, out of things they say when they think the check is settled.

  • A biker has asked the woman behind the bar out four times over the years. She has never once remembered him.
  • Somebody came a very long way to collect a specimen from that lounge. The report was never filed. They’re friends now.
  • One slice of pie. Eleven coffee refills. A Tuesday. She wrote it down. She writes everything down.
  • Now and then a second motorcycle comes up the road. It’s the wrong make, and there’s a reason for that.
  • There is a roadrunner. Nobody has caught it.

Some of it only happens when it rains. Some of it only happens if you leave them alone for a while.

It knows what it’s like outside.

Leave the weather on and the sky in the room is the sky over your head. The highway goes gold at sunset and dark after it. The windows of the diner go with the hour. Somebody mentions the forecast, usually unhelpfully.

What it will never do.

  • No ads. Not one, not ever, not “just a small banner at the bottom.”
  • No tracking. Nothing about you is collected. There is no server to send it to.
  • No subscription. No pro tier, no trial, no feature dangled out of reach.
  • No tricks. No fake urgency, no buried buttons, no guilt on the way out.
I know it’s none of my business what you spent or who you were with. You have enough people trying to trick you out of your hard-earned money. I’d rather not be one of them. — Ryan, who made it

There’s a tip jar at the bottom of Settings. It unlocks nothing, because there’s nothing left to unlock. The app never asks.

Common questions

How do I type a bill?
The keypad works right to left, like a cash register. 4237 is $42.37.
Why did my total go up a few cents?
Out of the box the total rounds up to the next dollar. The TOTAL line says "ROUNDED ↑" when that happens, and the TIP line shows what you actually left. Change it in Settings ▸ Rounding.
The Open Road seems to praise small tips.
It does. That character is cheap and his scale runs backwards on purpose. Every other room reads a tip the normal way.
Where did my souvenir photo go?
To your camera roll, if you allowed Photos access when asked. You can make it again any time: swipe or press and hold a stop in the Road Log and choose Souvenir.
Can I get my data out?
Settings ▸ Export writes the whole ledger as CSV into a folder you own, reachable from the Files app under On My iPhone ▸ Hot Tip.
Does the app track me or show ads?
No, and it never will. See the privacy policy.

Common questions

How do I type a bill?
The keypad works right to left, like a cash register. 4237 is $42.37.
Why did my total go up a few cents?
Out of the box the total rounds up to the next dollar. The TOTAL line says "ROUNDED ↑" when that happens, and the TIP line shows what you actually left. Change it in Settings ▸ Rounding.
The Open Road seems to praise small tips.
It does. That character is cheap and his scale runs backwards on purpose. Every other room reads a tip the normal way.
Where did my souvenir photo go?
To your camera roll, if you allowed Photos access when asked. You can make it again any time: swipe or press and hold a stop in the Road Log and choose Souvenir.
Can I get my data out?
Settings ▸ Your data ▸ Export writes the whole ledger — every run, stop, person, expense, milestone and photo — as a .hottip package (a ledger.json plus a photos folder) into a folder you own: iCloud Drive ▸ Hot Tip when iCloud Drive is on, otherwise On My iPhone ▸ Hot Tip. A backup of the same package is refreshed there automatically whenever you leave the app with something new. To move to a new phone or take a friend's rides, tap the package in Files, or use Settings ▸ Import — it adds what is missing and never deletes.
Does the app track me or show ads?
No ads, no analytics, no tracking and no account, and it will stay that way. See the privacy policy.
Why does the app ask for my location and camera?
Location does two jobs, both only while the app is open: it stamps the town, county and coordinates on a road-stop souvenir, and — at reduced accuracy — it lets the rooms ask Apple Weather what it is like outside, so the highway can go gold at sunset and rain can show through the diner window. Your location goes to Apple for that answer and nowhere else; it is never stored and never sent to the developer. Turn it off in Settings ▸ Outside. The camera is only for photographing a receipt, a place or a plate, and that photo stays in the app's own store on your phone.
What does the app cost?
Nothing. Every room, every feature and every update is included. Nothing is locked, nothing is a trial, and nothing expires. There is a busker's case at the bottom of Settings if you want to leave something — it buys nothing, unlocks nothing, and the app never asks.

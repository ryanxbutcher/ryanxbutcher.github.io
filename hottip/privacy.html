Hot Tip — Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 September 2026

Hot Tip has no account, no server of its own, no analytics and no advertising. Your ledger stays on your iPhone. One feature — the rooms reacting to the weather outside — asks Apple's weather service about your area, and that is described below.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your checks, the people you log them to, your trips and stops, and any photos you attach are saved in a database on your own iPhone.
  • None of it is uploaded to the developer. There is no Hot Tip account and no Hot Tip server to upload it to.
  • When you save or share a souvenir, that is your own action through iOS. The app never posts anything anywhere for you.
  • You can export the whole ledger — every record and photo — as a package (a JSON file plus the photos) and take it with you. When iCloud Drive is on, the app also keeps a backup of that package in your iCloud Drive, under Hot Tip; with it off, the backup stays on the phone. Either way it is your storage, not ours, and it never passes through us.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it kept on the phone. A backup already written to your iCloud Drive is yours and stays there until you delete it, the same as any other file in your Drive.

The weather in the rooms

The rooms can react to what it is like outside — the highway goes gold around sunset, rain shows through the diner window, somebody mentions the forecast. To do that the app asks Apple Weather about your area, the same service the Weather app uses.

  • The request uses a reduced-accuracy location, not your exact position, and it goes from your phone to Apple. It does not come to the developer, and there is nowhere else for it to go.
  • It happens at most once every few hours, and only while the app is open on a theme that uses it.
  • What comes back — the sky, the sunset, an alert headline and three days of conditions — is held only for the moment it is used. No coordinates are written down, and nothing is attached to your ledger.
  • You can turn it off in Settings ▸ Outside. Off, the rooms simply don't know, and nothing else changes.

Permissions the app may ask for

  • Camera — only when you photograph a receipt, a place or a plate. The photo is downscaled and saved into the app's own database on your device. The camera is not used for anything else.
  • Photos (add only) — so a trip souvenir can be saved to your camera roll. The app can add a photo and cannot read the photos already there.
  • Location (while using the app) — used in two places, and only while the app is open. When you log a road-trip stop, one position fix is taken so the town, county and coordinates can be printed on that stop's souvenir; that stays on your phone, and the lookup that turns coordinates into a place name is performed by Apple's own system service. Separately, a reduced-accuracy location is used to ask Apple Weather what it is like outside, as described above. There is no tracking, no background location, and no location history beyond the stops you save.
  • Face ID — only if you turn on Dogstar Mode, which puts a lock in front of your ledger. The result is checked by iOS; the app never sees biometric data.
  • Contacts — the app uses Apple's contact picker, which runs outside the app. It receives only the one contact you tap and never has access to your address book.

What is never done

Nothing about you is sold, rented, or handed to a data broker. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no third-party tracking of any kind in this app.

The tip jar

Hot Tip is free and complete. If you choose to leave a tip, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store — the developer never sees your payment details, and the app does not record who tipped or whether you did. Nothing in the app is locked behind it.

Children

Hot Tip is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

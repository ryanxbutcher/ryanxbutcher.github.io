Hot Tip for iPhone

Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 September 2026

Hot Tip does not collect your data. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you type stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your checks, the people you log them to, your trips and stops, and any photos you attach are saved in a database on your own iPhone.
  • Nothing is uploaded to the developer. There is no Hot Tip server to upload it to.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of it. You can also export the whole ledger as a CSV file and take it with you.

Permissions the app may ask for

  • Camera — only when you photograph a receipt or a place. The photo is saved into the app's own database.
  • Photos (add only) — so a trip souvenir can be saved to your camera roll. The app can add a photo and cannot read the photos already there.
  • Location (while using the app) — one position fix while you are logging a road-trip stop, so the town, county and coordinates can be printed on that stop's souvenir. Turning coordinates into a place name is done by Apple's own system service. There is no tracking, no background location, and no location history beyond the stop you saved.
  • Face ID — only if you turn on Boss Key, which puts a lock in front of your ledger. The result is checked by iOS; the app never sees biometric data.
  • Contacts — the app uses Apple's contact picker, which runs outside the app. It receives only the one contact you tap and never has access to your address book.

Children

Hot Tip is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
